Nafile is a modern-arabesque display typeface with a wide, versatile range: six widths from ExtraCondensed to Expanded and weights from Thin to Black, all in a single variable font.

The project was born in Barcelona, where the first sketches were drawn. The city's Gothic roots, Catalan modernism, Mediterranean charm and touches of Arabesque, all blended into its modern rhythm, became the heart of the design.

To contribute, please see github.com/ertekinno/nafile.
